AI Airplanes

Unity
C#
ML-Agents
The Game I developed a game that combines aviation and AI, where players navigate an airplane through a challenging checkpoint course while racing against AI-controlled planes. The AI planes were trained using Unity's ML-Agents, a toolkit that leverages reinforcement learning (PPO) to create intelligent agents. The Approach This project allowed me to combine game development and machine learning, focusing on designing effective observations and reward systems to optimize AI performance/learning. It was an exciting opportunity to research this topic, showcasing the capabilities of AI in a game related environment. Technical Details
  • Observations: velocity, checkpoint position/direction, raycasts
  • Rewards: checkpoint progress vs. collisions and idling
  • Training: PPO, monitored through TensorBoard
