The Research In my free time, I've been researching Unity DOTS and data-oriented programming, to get a deeper understanding of how it works under the hood and how it compares to Unity's traditional GameObject-based approach.

Alongside the research, I've been building a small game designed to benefit from DOTS's performance gains, learning the framework hands-on as I go. Once the core gameplay is in place, I'm planning to add DOTS's multiplayer netcode for a co-op mode. Career Impact This side project turned out to be more than just research, it directly shaped my career. The hands-on understanding of data-oriented design and ECS I built up here is what helped me land two jobs:

  • The Pack, a freelance role working on their Unity DOTS-based game, where I could contribute to the ECS codebase from day one
  • Vintecc, a role where the entire architecture was built around a custom DOD/ECS system with its own dependency injection framework; understanding data-oriented design and ECS at its core made a real difference there

The project itself has been on hold since, mostly because I've been getting hands-on ECS/DOD experience in the field instead, which, in a way, is this research paying off exactly as intended.
